Artificial Intelligence is a broad field focused on making machines behave intelligently.
Machine Learning is a subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data instead of being explicitly programmed.
Deep Learning is a further subset of ML that uses neural networks with many layers.
Data Science focuses on extracting insights from data using statistics, programming, and domain knowledge.
AI focuses on decision making, while Data Science focuses on understanding data.
All four fields are interconnected but serve different purposes.
